# PulseLocal AI — Sales Playbook

How to pick who to pitch, what to say, and how to use the app to close local businesses abroad from India.

---

## 1. The one-line pitch

> "Every missed call and slow Instagram reply is a booking that goes to your competitor. PulseLocal answers in 15 seconds, books the job, and gets you more 5-star Google reviews, for about a quarter of what Podium charges."

Three outcomes, in the owner's language:

| You say | They hear |
| --- | --- |
| Missed-call text-back in 15 seconds | "I stop losing jobs while I'm busy." |
| AI replies on Instagram & website, books into the calendar | "Leads get handled at 10 pm without me." |
| Review booster + negative review shield | "My Google rating goes up, bad reviews stay private." |

## 2. Who to pitch (target audience)

### The ideal customer

- **Owner-operated local service business**, 1–15 staff, one or two locations.
- **The owner does the work** (injecting, cleaning teeth, detailing cars, fixing AC), so they physically cannot answer the phone during jobs.
- **High-value jobs**: one extra booking a month pays for the whole plan.
- **Gets enquiries by phone and Instagram**, books by phone or a basic booking link.
- **Has a Google listing** with a rating between ~3.8 and 4.6, or fewer than ~150 reviews (room to grow).
- **English-speaking, card-paying market**: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland.

### The four niches the app is built for (ranked for a beginner)

| Rank | Niche | Average job (demo) | Why they buy | Best channel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Auto detailing / ceramic coating** | $540 | Owner is literally washing cars, phone in the van. Very Instagram-heavy. Fast decisions. | Instagram DM |
| 2 | **MedSpa / aesthetics** | $312 | Lots of DMs ("how much is Botox?"), no-shows hurt, reviews matter a lot. | Instagram DM + email |
| 3 | **HVAC, plumbing, roofing** | $385 | Emergency calls. A missed call = the customer calls the next company within minutes. | Email + phone |
| 4 | **Dental** | $410 | High value, but front-desk staff and practice managers slow the decision down. | Email to practice manager |

Start with **one niche in one country** for your first 2–3 weeks. Your scripts get sharper and your demo stories become specific.

### Strong buying signals (pitch these first)

The Lead finder scores most of these automatically:

- **No website**, or a website with no booking button.
- **Rating under 4.5**, or a recent 1–2★ review complaining about "never called back" / "couldn't get through".
- **Under 50–100 reviews** while competitors nearby have 300+.
- **Posts on Instagram regularly** but comments asking prices go unanswered for hours.
- **"Call for a quote" / "DM to book"** in the bio.
- **Hiring a receptionist** (job posts on Indeed): they already feel the pain and budget is there.

### Who NOT to pitch

- Franchises and chains (decisions are made at head office).
- Businesses already using Podium, Birdeye or a full GoHighLevel setup (hard switch, low margin for you).
- Businesses with a 4.9★ rating and 800 reviews (no obvious pain).
- Anyone you can't contact legally (see section 9).

### Who is the decision maker

| Niche | Talk to | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Detailing, HVAC, roofing | The owner (often the name on the Google listing or Instagram) | Generic info@ inboxes if you can find the owner |
| MedSpa | Owner or lead injector / nurse owner | Front desk |
| Dental | Practice manager or the owner dentist | Receptionist (they may feel replaced) |

## 5. How to pitch

### Golden rules

1. **Lead with their problem, not your product.** Never open with "I'm a developer from India who built an AI tool."
2. **Show, don't tell.** The branded demo link does the selling. Your message only needs to get the click.
3. **Short.** DMs under 60 words, emails under 120 words.
4. **One ask.** "Take a 60-second look" or "Worth a 10-minute call?" Never both.
5. **Specific numbers.** "4.1★ from 37 reviews" beats "your reviews could be better."
6. **Speed wins.** Follow up within an hour of the "opened your demo" alert.

### Sending times from India (IST)

Aim for 8–10 am in their time zone:

| Market | Their 8–10 am is roughly | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US East (New York, Miami) | 5:30–8:30 pm IST | Shifts by 1 hour with US daylight saving |
| US Central (Austin, Chicago) | 6:30–9:30 pm IST | |
| US West (LA, Phoenix) | 8:30–11:30 pm IST | |
| UK / Ireland | 12:30–3:30 pm IST | Shifts with UK daylight saving |
| Australia East (Sydney) | ~2:30–5:30 am IST | Schedule emails the night before |

Tuesday to Thursday works best. Avoid Monday mornings and weekends for trades.

> Hey {first name}, quick one: when someone DMs about {top service} while you're with a client, how fast do they get a reply?
>
> I put {business} into a live demo of an AI front desk (answers DMs + missed calls, books, asks for Google reviews):
> {tracked link}
>
> 60 seconds. If it's not useful, ignore me 🙂

The Pitch kit generates this automatically with their name, service and tracked link.

### Follow-ups (stop as soon as they reply)

| When | Message |
| --- | --- |
| **Within 1 hour of "opened your demo"** | "Saw you had a look at the demo, {first name}. Did the missed-call text-back part make sense? Happy to set it up with your real prices so you can test it on your own phone." |
| **Day 3, no open** | "Bumping this in case it got buried. Here's the 60-sec demo built for {business}: {link}" |
| **Day 7** | "One stat from your area: {competitor} has {X} reviews vs your {Y}. The review booster in the demo is how we close that gap. Want me to turn it on for a free week?" |
| **Day 14 (last)** | "Last one from me. If now's not the time, no problem. Should I check back in 3 months?" |

Log each touch in the Pipeline (one click) so follow-up dates set themselves.

### Script 3 — WhatsApp (only after they replied or shared their number)

> Hi {first name}, it's {your name} from {brand}. Here's the demo for {business} again: {link}. Want me to set it up with your real prices? Takes me about 48 hours.

### Script 4 — Loom video (2–3 minutes, for warm leads)

1. **0:00 Hook:** "{First name}, I built this as if {business} already used it."
2. **0:15 Dashboard:** point at Revenue recovered and Missed calls saved.
3. **0:40 Missed-call demo:** run it live.
4. **1:10 AI chat:** ask "how much for {top service}?" It quotes their price.
5. **1:40 Reviews:** show 5★ → Google, 2★ → private.
6. **2:10 Pricing page:** "${growth}/mo vs Podium's ~${podium}. Live in 48 hours. Link's below."

The Pitch kit has this script personalised too.

### The 10-minute sales call

1. **(1 min) Context:** "What made you open the demo?"
2. **(3 min) Pain questions:**
   - "Roughly how many calls do you miss in a busy week?"
   - "How long does an Instagram DM usually wait for a reply?"
   - "What's an average job worth?"
   - "How do you ask for Google reviews today?"
3. **(1 min) Do the maths out loud** using the ROI calculator: "Even 2 extra jobs a month at ${ticket} is ${2×ticket}. The plan is ${price}."
4. **(3 min) Show** the one demo part that matches their biggest pain.
5. **(2 min) Close:** "I can have it live by {day}. Shall I send the link for the Growth plan?" Then stop talking.

### Objection handling

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "Too expensive." | "One extra booking a month covers it. If it doesn't pay for itself in 30 days, cancel, no contract." |
| "I already have a receptionist." | "Great. This covers evenings, weekends, lunch breaks and when she's on another call. It also handles reviews, which receptionists rarely chase." |
| "I'm not into AI / customers want a human." | "It only handles the first reply and booking. Anything it's unsure about goes straight to you. Customers just see a fast, polite reply." |
| "We use Podium/Birdeye." | "What are you paying now? This does the core of it for ${price}. Happy to run both for a week and compare." |
| "Send me more info." | "Sure. The demo link *is* the info, built for {business}. Which part should I walk you through, missed calls or reviews?" |
| "You're in India?" | "Yes, which is why it's ${price} and not $400. Setup and support are on WhatsApp, and I'm online during your business hours." |
| "Not now." | "No problem. Can I check back in {month}? Busy season is usually when missed calls hurt most." Set a follow-up date in the Pipeline. |

### Pricing guidance

Set exact numbers in **Offer** (`/settings`). Defaults:

| Plan | Price | Who it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $49/mo | Solo operators, just missed-call text-back + chat |
| **Growth** (lead with this) | $79/mo | Most businesses: full desk + bookings + reviews |
| Premium | $99/mo | Busy shops, multiple staff calendars |

- Offer a **free 7-day trial** (or "first month 50% off") only to warm leads who opened the demo twice or more.

## 7. What to fix before your first paid customer (important)

The app is a **sales demo**. When someone pays, you must actually deliver missed-call text-back, AI replies and review requests for *their* business. Pick one of these before you start charging:

| Option | Speed | Cost to you | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. White-label platform** (e.g. GoHighLevel agency plan or similar) | Days | Monthly platform fee (check current price) | Has missed-call text-back, review requests, chat widget and calendars built in. You configure it per client. Fastest way to deliver. |
| **B. Build delivery into PulseLocal** (Twilio + an LLM API + Google review link) | 1–3 weeks dev | Per-message SMS + AI usage | Highest margin and fully your product. Recommended as the next build phase. |
| **C. Manual "concierge" start** | Immediate | Your time | For the first 1–3 clients: set up call forwarding + an SMS auto-reply app + a review link sheet. Only to learn; does not scale. |

US texting needs **A2P 10DLC registration** through your SMS provider (takes days to weeks), so start this early for US clients.

## 8. What to build next (roadmap)

In order of impact on revenue:

1. **Client delivery (Option B above):** real Twilio number per client, missed-call text-back, AI replies using their service list, booking link, automatic review requests. Without this you can sell but not deliver.
2. **Email sending + automatic follow-ups from the app:** send the cold email and the day 3 / 7 / 14 follow-ups automatically, stop when they reply or open the demo.
3. **Deploy online** (Render + MongoDB Atlas, README has the steps) so tracked links work from anywhere.
4. **Client portal:** a login for paying customers to see their missed calls saved, bookings and new reviews each month (reduces cancellations).
5. **Agency website** for your brand with case studies once you have 2–3 clients.

## 9. Rules to stay safe (check the latest rules yourself; this is not legal advice)

- **USA (CAN-SPAM):** cold B2B email is allowed if you use a truthful subject, your real name, a physical postal address, and honour opt-outs quickly.
- **UK (PECR + UK GDPR):** emailing companies (limited companies, LLPs) is generally allowed with an opt-out. Sole traders and partnerships are treated like individuals and need consent, so be careful with one-person businesses.
- **Canada (CASL):** stricter. You can email an address that is published publicly *and* relevant to their business role, with no "no unsolicited email" notice. Always include an unsubscribe and your contact details.
- **Australia (Spam Act):** similar to Canada. Publicly published business addresses relevant to the offer, plus identification and unsubscribe.
- **SMS/WhatsApp:** never cold-text. Only message on WhatsApp after they've replied or given you their number.
- **Instagram:** no automated DM tools. Send manually.
- Keep a "do not contact" note in the Pipeline (mark as **Lost** with a note) and never message them again.
